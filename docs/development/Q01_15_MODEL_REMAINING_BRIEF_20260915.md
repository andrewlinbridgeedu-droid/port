Game-ready character reference, preserve clear shoulder elbow wrist hip knee ankle joints for rigging.

动作需求：呼吸式机械校准、双手合拢护甲、抬笔校正、前臂重击、受击、死亡；法术挂点为笔尖与胸前记录板。

## 精英空壳守卫（13关）
用途：强化防御后蓄力重击，恢复期形成可攻击窗口。与普通守卫保持同族，但轮廓、武器及动作不同。

建模提示：A full-body elite hollow archive executioner in dark fantasy armor, broad top-heavy silhouette, deep navy and aged pale gold plate armor, large asymmetric shoulder mantle of rigid segmented plates, a heavy two-handed execution maul held in a readable low ready pose, hollow helmet with a narrow warm amber slit, distinct layered mechanical torso, long articulated legs and visible joint gaps, worn engraved metal without readable letters, threatening planted stance with bent knees, high quality physically based sculpted surfaces, no cape hiding the skeleton, no sword, no environmental platform, no particles, no extra limbs. Clearly distinguish from an ordinary red-black sword guard and a white archive sentinel. Preserve hands and limb joints for animation.

动作需求：沉重呼吸/重心变化、武器逐渐抬起蓄力、重击、低头恢复、受击、死亡。实际攻击仍在远处完成，不更改已确认站位。
