# Archetype recipes

## Projectile

Use a cohesive moving core plus a short ribbon/echo and a front/back impact pair. The core must remain visibly one projectile throughout travel. Scale may grow toward the camera; do not substitute loose streaks for the projectile.

## Slash

Use one dominant blade silhouette, one or two torn echoes, and contact layers. Travel with continuous interpolation. Avoid a bundle of disconnected brush cards.

## Burst

Anchor at impact. Expand an irregular dense mass in depth, then decay with smoke/debris. Avoid perfect circles and flat starbursts unless the brief explicitly calls for geometry.

## Beam

Keep a stable origin-to-target axis, readable core width, restrained glow, and terminal impact. Avoid a single transparent line.

## Aura

Bind to a live actor anchor. Use layered orbit/updraft/ground response with bounded lifetime. Preserve actor readability outside the intended peak engulfing beat.

## Storm

Use a broad environmental volume, directional flow, ground contact, and a readable center of force. Avoid many independent translucent sheets.

## Summon

The summoned silhouette must articulate or deform over time. Use an authored sprite sequence, animated prefab, Effekseer model, or `ISpellExtension`; never move one static illustration across the screen and call it animation.
